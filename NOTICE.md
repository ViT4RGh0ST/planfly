## About the money, in plain words

planfly computes figures from exchange rates it gets from sources you connect,
or that you type in by hand. None of those figures is financial, accounting or
tax advice, and none of them serves as a receipt for anybody.

A source can publish a wrong figure, go down, or change without warning. The
program is written so that this shows — it flags what it could not value and
says how old each rate is — but **checking your own figures is your
responsibility**. Before making a decision with real money, verify them.
